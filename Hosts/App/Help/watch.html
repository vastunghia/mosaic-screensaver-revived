Watch the mosaic

The Mosaic window shows the effect as it runs. You can also watch it full screen, or let it run as your screen saver.

Full screen

Click the mosaic in the window, click Play, or choose Controls › Play (⌘↩). Press any key or click to come back.

Full screen needs access to your Photos library when that is where the photographs come from: it shows larger versions of them as the zoom gets close.

As your screen saver

Open System Settings › Screen Saver and choose Mosaic, under Other. On macOS 26 the screen savers are behind the Screen Saver button of System Settings › Wallpaper. In the Mosaic window, Open Screen Saver Settings… takes you there.

The screen saver shows the mosaic on your main display; other displays stay black.
