Uninstall Mosaic

Quit Mosaic, then remove the app, the screen saver and the copies of your photographs.

  1. Choose Mosaic › Quit Mosaic. If Mosaic asks, click Quit.
  2. Move these to the Bin:
    • /Applications/Mosaic.app
    • ~/Library/Screen Savers/Mosaic.saver
    • ~/Library/Application Support/Mosaic
  3. If Mosaic is still listed in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions, remove it.

Your photographs and your Photos library are not affected.
