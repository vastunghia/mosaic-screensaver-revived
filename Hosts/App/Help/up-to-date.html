Keep the photographs up to date

While Mosaic opens at login, it runs in the background and prepares new photographs as your library changes, so the screen saver keeps up.

Open at login

Mosaic turns this on when you install the screen saver. You can turn it off in Mosaic Settings, under In the Background. If macOS asks you to approve it, click Open Login Items… and turn Mosaic on in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions.

Close the window, don’t quit

Closing the Mosaic window leaves Mosaic running in the background, without an icon in the Dock. Quitting stops it until you next log in, and until then the screen saver doesn’t pick up new photographs. That is why Mosaic asks before it quits; Close Window is what keeps it running.

Update now

To prepare new photographs straight away, choose Controls › Update Photographs (⌘R). Mosaic doesn’t watch a folder of photographs as it watches the Photos library: after you change the folder, update it this way, or wait for the next login.
