Mosaic Help

Mosaic turns your photographs into an endless zoom: every photograph turns out to be one tile of a mosaic, which makes up another photograph, which is one tile of the next mosaic.

It is the Mosaic screen saver from Mac OS X Leopard, rebuilt for today’s macOS. The app prepares your photographs; the screen saver shows them.
