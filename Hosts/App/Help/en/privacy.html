Your photographs and privacy

Your photographs never leave your Mac. Mosaic Companion keeps small copies of them, and the screen saver reads only those.

What Mosaic Companion keeps

For each photograph the screen saver uses, Mosaic Companion keeps a small copy to build mosaics from and a larger one for close-ups, in ~/Library/Application Support/Mosaic. That takes about 1.2 GB for 2,048 photographs, and about 1 GB more for a while each time Mosaic chooses other photographs (Change photographs). It never changes your photographs.

If your library keeps originals in iCloud, Mosaic Companion downloads nothing unless you choose to, in the setup or in Settings. With Sharp close-ups only, it asks Photos for a sharper copy of the photographs it shows full screen, and of some it shows large around them, about 1 MB each, at most 256 a day and 512 a week; Photos keeps those copies in your library until it needs the space.

Where a photograph has faces, Mosaic Companion finds them on your Mac, so that its copy keeps them in the picture. Nothing is sent anywhere.

To leave out screenshots, documents and weak photos, Mosaic Companion reads the scores Photos gives your photographs from the Photos library’s own database, read only, and has your Mac look at the photographs Photos has not scored yet. Everything stays on your Mac, and nothing in your library changes. To turn this off, see Settings.

What the screen saver shows

The screen saver shows your photographs while you are away from your Mac, to whoever passes by. From the Photos library it uses every photograph, or every photograph in the albums you choose, except those you have hidden in Photos and those in Recently Deleted; from a folder, every image in it. So a photograph you would rather keep private can fill the screen. To keep such photographs off it:

  • hide them in Photos;
  • choose only some albums, in Settings, such as one you put together for Mosaic;
  • or use a folder of photographs you picked.

Access to Photos

Only Mosaic Companion reads your Photos library, with the access you give it. The screen saver never asks for it and never reads the library: it uses the copies the app made. To change the access, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

Checking for updates

Once a day Mosaic Companion asks GitHub, where it is published, whether there is a newer version, and downloads it from there. GitHub sees your IP address, as it would on a visit to the page. Nothing about you or your photographs is sent. See Update Mosaic.
