If something doesn’t work

Problems with Photos access, missing photographs and the screen saver, and how to solve them.

Mosaic can’t read the Photos library

If the window says Access was refused., click Open Privacy Settings…, turn Mosaic on under Photos, then click Reopen Mosaic: the access starts with the next launch. You can also use a folder of photographs instead.

If it says Limited to the photographs you picked, Mosaic sees only those. Click Change… to give it the whole library.

Some photographs are missing

Under the progress of Preparation, Mosaic counts the photographs it left out. It takes others in their place, until it has as many as you chose in Mosaic Settings; if it has fewer, your library has no more it can use.

only in iCloud
Their originals aren’t on this Mac, and neither is a copy large enough. If too few photographs are ready, you can turn on Download photographs kept only in iCloud in Mosaic Settings.
only as tiles
Photos keeps only a small copy of them on this Mac. Mosaic uses them as tiles, but never shows them full screen; it makes sure at least a quarter of the photographs can fill the screen, as long as your library has that many.
unreadable
The files couldn’t be opened as images, for example because they are damaged or in a format macOS can’t read.

The mosaic doesn’t appear

Mosaic needs at least 100 photographs ready. If your albums or folder have fewer, choose more in Mosaic Settings.

The screen saver isn’t in System Settings

Look under Other, at the end of the list. On macOS 26 the list is behind the Screen Saver button of Wallpaper. If it isn’t there, open Mosaic and check the Screen Saver step; click Show Setup Steps if the steps are hidden.

If the step says The installed screen saver is not this version., click Update. This happens after you update the app.

The screen saver shows old photographs

Mosaic updates them only while it runs. See Keep the photographs up to date.

Other displays are black

Mosaic shows the mosaic on the main display only.
