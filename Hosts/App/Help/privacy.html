Your photographs and privacy

Your photographs never leave your Mac. Mosaic keeps small copies of them, and the screen saver reads only those.

What Mosaic keeps

For each photograph it uses, Mosaic keeps a small copy to build mosaics from and a larger one for close-ups, in ~/Library/Application Support/Mosaic. That takes about 1.2 GB for 2,048 photographs. Mosaic never changes your photographs.

If your library keeps originals in iCloud, Mosaic downloads nothing unless you choose to in Mosaic Settings. With Sharp close-ups only, it asks Photos for a sharper copy of the photographs it shows full screen, and of some it shows large around them, about 1 MB each, at most 256 a day and 512 a week; Photos keeps those copies in your library until it needs the space.

Where a photograph has faces, Mosaic finds them on your Mac, so that its copy keeps them in the picture. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Access to Photos

Only the Mosaic app reads your Photos library, with the access you give it. The screen saver never asks for it and never reads the library: it uses the copies the app made. To change the access, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

Checking for updates

Once a day Mosaic asks GitHub, where it is published, whether there is a newer version, and downloads it from there. GitHub sees your IP address, as it would on a visit to the page. Nothing about you or your photographs is sent. See Update Mosaic.
