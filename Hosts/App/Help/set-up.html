Set up Mosaic

The Mosaic window takes you through a few steps, in order. Each one shows a check mark when it is done.

Applications

This step appears only if Mosaic is running from where it was downloaded. Click Move to Applications: Mosaic opens again from there. The screen saver and opening at login need the app to stay in one place.

On an account that is not an administrator, macOS asks for an administrator’s name and password first, as the Finder would. Each update moved there later asks again.

Photographs

Choose where the photographs come from: Photos library or A folder.

  • For the Photos library, click Allow Access… and allow Mosaic to access your photos.
  • For a folder, click Choose… and pick it. Mosaic includes the folders inside it too.

You need at least 100 photographs; a couple of thousand look best.

Preparation

Mosaic makes a small copy of each photograph to build the mosaics from. This starts by itself and can take a while, depending on how many photographs you use. The mosaic appears in the window, and the screen saver works, as soon as 100 are ready. You can click Stop at any time: Mosaic carries on from where it stopped the next time.

Screen Saver

Click Install…. Mosaic installs its screen saver and opens the Screen Saver settings: choose Mosaic, under Other.

Installing the screen saver also sets Mosaic to open at login, without a window, so that the screen saver keeps up with your library. See Keep the photographs up to date.

Ready

When every step is done, the window shows the mosaic and a short summary instead of the steps. To see the steps again, click Show Setup Steps.
