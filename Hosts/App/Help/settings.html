Change Mosaic Settings

Choose which photographs Mosaic uses, how many rows each mosaic has, how long each zoom lasts, and whether Mosaic opens at login.

Choose Mosaic › Settings… (⌘,), or click Mosaic Settings… under the mosaic. The screen saver uses the same settings.

Source

Choose Photos library or A folder. Changing the source prepares the photographs again, from the start.

Photographs to Use

These appear when the photographs come from your Photos library.

Albums
Click Choose… to pick albums and folders, as Photos shows them in its sidebar. With none chosen, Mosaic uses the whole library; with several, it combines them. A chosen folder includes any album you add to it later.
Which photographs
When there are more photographs than Mosaic uses, this decides which ones. Spread over time takes an even share from every month of your library; Most recent takes the latest; Random takes a sample of everything.
How many
From 512 to 8,192. More photographs give mosaics more variety, and take longer to prepare and more space on disk. 2,048 is a good balance. When one of the photographs chosen can’t be used, Mosaic takes another in its place, so you get this many as long as your library has them. If Photos keeps only a small copy of some on this Mac, Mosaic uses those as tiles, and makes sure at least one in sixteen can fill the screen, as long as your library has that many.
Download photographs kept only in iCloud
If your library keeps originals in iCloud, Mosaic can download them to prepare them. When this is off, Mosaic uses what Photos keeps on this Mac and leaves the rest out, taking other photographs in their place. Downloading can take a long time and a lot of space on disk: turn it on only if too few photographs are ready without it. Even with this off, Mosaic downloads a sharper copy of the photographs it shows full screen when this Mac doesn’t have one, so that close-ups aren’t blurry: about 2 MB each, at most 128 a day and 256 a week. It never downloads originals, and it prefers photographs whose sharper copy is already on this Mac.

Mosaic

Rows
How many rows of tiles each mosaic has, from 60 to 120. More rows mean smaller tiles and a finer picture.
Zoom duration
How long it takes to zoom out from one photograph to the next, from 10 to 60 seconds.

In the Background

Open at login keeps Mosaic running without a window, so that the screen saver keeps up with your library. See Keep the photographs up to date.

Below it, whether Mosaic may send notifications, which it uses only to say that a new version is ready. See Update Mosaic.
